id Software has created an ftp site at: ftp.idsoftware.com.
The corresponding fsp site is fsp.idsoftware.com, port 21.

This will be the first site to see any new products published by
id, such as Strife and Quake.  It has a very recent snapshot of
the ftp.cdrom.com site and encourages public uploads.  The connection
sports a dedicated T1 and a current limit of 50 simultaneous ftp
users, and buttloads of fsp users.  There is currently 300+Mb of
Doom and Heretic material on there with about 150Mb free.  We will
be adding a few more Gb of free space next month.
