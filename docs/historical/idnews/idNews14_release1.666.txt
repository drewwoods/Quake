DOOM v1.666 has been uploaded to ftp.uwp.edu.  Though it is currently
in /pub/incoming/id, it should shortly be in /pub/msdos/games/id.
It is also at Software Creations BBS and is on its way to CompuServe
soon.

The files: DM1666SP.ZIP - shareware wad patch
           DM1666SW.ZIP - executable
           DM1666RP.ZIP - registered wad patch

Most of you will want the first two files.  Our kind, wonderful,
gracious customers will want the third file.

In the long-standing tradition of the DOOM sound code, it is still
not stable.  There's something majorly wrong with the PAS sound
code right now.  If you own a PAS of any sort and want to do us a
favor, please check it out and drop a note to pjr@hnv.com (the
sound library guy) with whatever results you get.  Thanks!

Enjoy.
