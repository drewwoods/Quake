Hi there, everyone.  Sorry for the long silence.  Things have been
very ____ around here (I think you know how to fill in the blank
by now).

Have a couple of updates for you and a VERY important request at
the end.

On v1.3..

  We're close.  The important parts of the sound code were totally
  rewritten for stability, especially in net games.  Our results
  so far have been very promising.  However, the GUS code was
  totally removed and needs to be rewritten as well.  As soon as
  we get that finished and Doom tested, we'll send it out.  Best
  estimate is a couple of weeks.  v1.3 also integrates fixes
  submitted by y'all to make the serial and net code more compatible
  and work faster.  There were a couple of savegame bugs fixed and
  a minor sky texture mapping bug fixed.  It also has a new map
  reload feature for the map editor types out there so that you
  can test a level immediately after developing it without restarting.

On ports of Doom..

  We're currently very ____ with the Jaguar ports of Wolf 3D and
  Doom and haven't had time to round out the Linux/X port and other
  stuff.  The Linux port should be closely followed by an Irix/X
  port and later by lots of other ports, but we haven't committed
  to release dates because we kinda figured a nasty crunch like
  this would come along.  I'm afraid that we still can't commit
  for the same reason.

On the Jaguar ports..

  We're doing Doom and Wolfenstein 3D for the Atari Jaguar.  They're
  both coming right along.  Wolf 3D is sporting several new features,
  including source artwork at twice the resolution (4x the data),
  a high, fluid refresh rate, 22 kHz sound effects, and MIDI device
  emulation.  Doom pushes the hardware a lot harder and already
  looks trey cool.  The anti-aliasing effects of composite screens
  coupled with the very hip 16-bit CRY pixel mode makes images look
  even more realistic than the DOS version.  Both should be done
  this summer.

On commercial Doom..

  In progress is the development of lotsa new everything for the
  commercial Doom release.  Will probably be done in September.
  We've got some hip new stuff for you like awesome new levels
  based on Earth, and several new bad guys, including possessed
  humans carrying chain guns, spider daemon offspring, and several
  new arch-daemons with extremely creative and grim methods of
  destruction.  We're paying even closer attention to the design
  of good deathmatch levels.

Our VERY important request..

  We could use your help.  Apparently, there was a game called "GATE
  OF DOOM" that came out some time ago.  When we applied for a
  trademark on "DOOM", they wouldn't give it to us because they
  thought the name "DOOM" was too close to the name "GATE OF DOOM"
  which has a trademark on it.

  Reasoning doesn't work.  Apparently, you have to do a petition
  to get people to say, "I associate Blammo's LOG! with this rotting
  lump of wood my daughter carries on her back everywhere with
  'LOG! from Blammo' written all over it."  Jay has written up a
  lusty e-mail letter to ask for your support.  If you've got a
  minute, it would help us a lot- thanks!
